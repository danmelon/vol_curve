No Arbitrage Conditions:

Two separate arbitrage types:

> Butterfly Arbitrage (violates within single expiry):
    > The implied risk-neutral density must be non-negative everywhere.
    > Derived via the Durreleman function g(k) -- Combination of w, w', w'' that must satisfy g(k)=> 0 for all k.
    > Also simple necessary condition from Roger Lee's moment formula: the asymptotic slopes of the wings are b(1+p) & b(1-p).
        > and both must be <= 4 in the T-scaled sense, or the wings imply negative density eventually.

> Calendar Arbitrage (violates across expiries):
    > Total variance at a fixed k must be non-decreasing in T. Intuitively, variance accumulates over time
        > it can't "decrease" as you look further out or you could construct a calendar spread that's a static arbitrage.
